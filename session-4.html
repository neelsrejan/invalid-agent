1. Session 4 · Fishbone, five Whys, and the 8D record

Architecture

rca.py turns the ranked hypothesis into a 6M fishbone, a five-step Why chain, and an 8D record with DMAIC labels. Every Why step needs a citation that exists in the catalog. A missing citation raises before markdown is written. Injections are visible only once their week has arrived.

2. Session 4 · A broken chain never becomes a file

Architecture

The RCA tests are the rule, not a tour of the markdown. A Why with no citation is an error. An unknown citation id is an error. On the walkthrough lot the top bone is material and the chain names that lot. The tests do not call Jira or a model.

How this session connects
